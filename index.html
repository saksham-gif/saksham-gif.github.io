Laser Turret Control System Embedded Systems

A 3D-printed microcontroller-based targeting turret with two modes — autonomous IR-sensor targeting and manual potentiometer control — built and programmed from scratch.

Designed and built a dual-mode targeting turret on the TM4C123GH6PM (ARM Cortex-M4). In Autonomous Mode, an IR sensor detects objects and drives a pan servo to track them. In Manual Mode, two potentiometers control pan and tilt independently. A DC motor drives a soft projectile launcher, triggered by a dedicated push button. A 16x2 LCD displays live mode, distance, and servo angles.

Authored the full Bill of Materials, wired every electrical connection on breadboard, and programmed all peripherals in C — ADC sampling at 100Hz, PWM for two servos and the launch motor, UART communication, GPIO interrupt-driven mode switching with 10ms debounce, and 4x hardware oversampling to eliminate sensor noise.

Automated Box Erecting & Packing Machine Mechatronics

A full-scale industrial automated packaging machine that erects flat cardboard blanks, fills them with exactly 6 M6 nuts, closes and seals the box — built by Team Sapphire for MENG-2500.

Designed and built an automated box packing system using an aluminum extrusion frame, pneumatic cylinders, dual conveyor belts, a solenoid-based nut dispenser, and relay ladder logic control. The machine pulls flat box blanks from a magazine using suction cups, opens and erects them, transports them via conveyor, dispenses exactly 6 M6 nuts using a solenoid gate with 6 tubes, folds all flaps closed, and applies a label seal.

My contributions included authoring the full BOM, SolidWorks CAD modeling and engineering drawings, pneumatic force calculations, and ladder logic design. The machine achieved 5 boxes/min throughput, 83% efficiency, and passed all validation tests including a 1-hour continuous run with zero critical failures.

Mechanical Clock Redesign Gear Systems

Redesigned a full mechanical clock from scratch — rebuilt the escapement mechanism, recalculated gear ratios across a 5–6 stage drivetrain, and improved timekeeping accuracy by ~40%.

Led a full redesign of a mechanical clock system, replacing the Swiss lever escapement with a standard escapement and rebuilding the pendulum and 5–6 gear drivetrain from the ground up. Through precise gear ratio calculations and alignment fixes, improved timekeeping accuracy by approximately 40%.

Mid-project, identified a spring mechanism failure that was causing the drivetrain to drift. Rather than patching around it, pivoted entirely to a gravity-driven weight system — solving the root cause and eliminating the drift through structured iterative testing. This required understanding how a change in one stage of the gear train propagated through every subsequent stage, the same systems thinking that applies to any precision mechanical environment.

Spacecraft Launch Simulation MATLAB / Physics Simulation

Built a physics-based rocket launch simulation in MATLAB — modelling thrust, gravity, and aerodynamic drag to visualize altitude, velocity, and trajectory over time.

Simulated rocket motion using Newton's second law (F = ma), a gravitational constant of 9.8 m/s², and drag force calculated as 0.5ρCdAV². Thrust was applied along a 60° launch angle. A simulation loop ran until the rocket landed (y < 0), storing position, velocity, and thrust at each timestep for plotting.

Generated trajectory, altitude, and velocity plots that matched real-world rocket motion expectations. The project demonstrated applied physics modelling, numerical simulation, and data visualization entirely in MATLAB — skills directly applicable to control systems and mechatronics engineering.
