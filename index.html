I like the whole path from idea to working machine: sizing the actuators, drawing it, wiring it, writing the firmware, and testing it until it holds up.

I'm a third-year Mechatronics Engineering student in the Robotics stream at Humber Polytechnic, working across embedded firmware in C, PLC and pneumatic automation, PCB design, and computer vision.

When something breaks, I want the root cause, not a patch. On my projects that has meant putting an oscilloscope on a misbehaving board, redesigning a failed mount, and rebuilding parts when a supplier fell through.

Outside class I co-founded and lead the Engineers Without Borders Humber chapter, mentor first-year students, and I'm teaching myself Python and computer vision with OpenCV.
